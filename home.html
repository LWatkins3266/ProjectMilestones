Edges First: About Me

My name is Lisa and I am from Memphis, Tennessee. I was born and raised here and currently still reside in my home town neighborhood. I have an older brother, one niece and nephew, 2 dogs and a cat. Some of my hobbies include writing, drawing, singing, cooking, and playing video games. My absolute favorites are cooking and gaming. I also like to watch anime and listen to music.

In a more professional light, I am a youth advocate and activist targeting issues around sexual health, rights, and awareness, gender equality, social justie, and self harm prevetion and awareness. I have been apart of many national organizations that tackle legal policy and social equality on a local, state, and national level. My works serves many communities and is inspired by my personal experiences. I pride myself on educatong others to create lasting and progressive change in our society.
